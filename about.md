> The first rule of the Data Science Club:

## Who are we?

Hi! We are the `Data Science Club` of the University of Rostock.
We meet once per week to tackle data science challenges on kaggle, learn new Machine Learning algorithms or just eat some pizza.
Everybody is welcome, so feel free to drop by!
Join us on Discord for more information.
